Help & Staff Guide

Step-by-step instructions for new staff.

1

Login

Enter the username and password assigned to authorized clinic staff. Do not share credentials.

2

Register an Appointment

Choose New Appointment, enter patient name, address, contact number, dentist, treatment, date and time, then click Save Appointment. The system creates a unique appointment number automatically.

3

Find Appointment Details

On the dashboard, type the appointment number into Find Appointment. The complete patient and appointment record is shown.

4

Calculate and Print a Bill

Open the appointment and choose Calculate & Print Bill. The total is calculated from the treatment fee plus the consultation fee. Use Print Receipt to print or save it as PDF.

5

Reports

Open Reports, select a date and view/print the daily appointment schedule. The dashboard and report page also show billed revenue.

6

Exit Safely

Use Exit / Logout. This invalidates the login session and returns to the secure login page.
